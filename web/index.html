Emerald Valley Station

A green rail hub over a forest valley: a 216 m timber gridshell on branching steel columns, a stream running between two elevated maglev decks, a canopy walk hung from the roof, vine curtains, wildflower roof gardens, solar glazing and a reflecting pool. Every image here is path-traced.
